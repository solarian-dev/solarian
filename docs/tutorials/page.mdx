# Tutorials

Five tutorials, in order. The first needs no hardware. From the second on, one project carries through: a Solarian Arm 1, a gripper, and a wrist camera. {{ className: 'lead' }}

- **[1. Gentle intro to Robotics](/docs/tutorials/gentle-intro).** The words the rest of the course uses: joints, coordinates, kinematics, the control loop, and learned policies. Nothing to install.
- **[2. Your first Robot](/docs/tutorials/first-robot).** A simulated Solarian Arm 1. You write the arm, install the gripper, add a wrist camera, and drive it from the keyboard.
- **[3. The real robot](/docs/tutorials/real-robot).** The same project on a desk: parts, assembly, a Raspberry Pi, calibration, and the keyboard on the real arm.
- **[4. Robot skills](/docs/tutorials/skills).** The keyboard stops nudging joints. A skill you write, MoveSkill from a plugin, and a pretrained UMI policy take its place.
- **[5. Train a skill](/docs/tutorials/training).** Train a UMI policy on an example dataset, then record your own demonstrations with a Lumi and train again.
