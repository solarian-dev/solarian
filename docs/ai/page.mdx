# AI Overview

Solarian's AI stack covers both levels of robot intelligence. At the low level, [Skills](/docs/skills) run learned [Policies](/docs/policies), typically diffusion, ACT or VLA models, that turn camera images and joint state into motor actions many times a second. At the high level, the [AI Agentic Controller](/docs/agentic_controller) wraps a vision-language model in an agent harness that gives the robot the ability to plan, decide, and act autonomously: it takes a task from the chat and carries it out by calling those skills as tools. Together they follow a **dual-process** design: the agentic controller decides *what* to do (System 2), and the learned policies handle *how* to do it (System 1). {{ className: 'lead' }}

## The pieces

<Properties>
  <Property name="AI Agentic Controller">
    Agentic control for any <a href="/docs/controllers">Controller</a>, enabled by an <code>agent:</code> block. A harness around any vision-language model (Anthropic, OpenAI, or any OpenAI-compatible server) takes a high-level task from the chat: a planner model plans it and carries it out by calling the controller's Skills as tools, while a fast monitor model watches each one through the cameras. See <a href="/docs/agentic_controller">AI Agentic Controller</a>.
  </Property>
  <Property name="Skills">
    Self-contained, restartable behaviors that run in their own process — a learned policy, a motion plan, a scripted routine. They are the agentic controller's tools, and they can also be started by the operator from a key or a button. See <a href="/docs/skills">Skills</a>.
  </Property>
  <Property name="Policies">
    The trainable models a Skill calls to produce actions (diffusion, ACT, VLAs, classical methods). Policies are trained and served independently of the Skill that consumes them. See <a href="/docs/policies">Policies</a>.
  </Property>
</Properties>

## System 1 and System 2

- **System 2 — the agentic controller.** Slow and deliberative. The planner runs in its own thread on the controller and wakes only when something changes (a message, an action ending, a monitor flag). It reads the task, what happened and the newest camera images, calls a VLM, and starts or stops Skills through tool calls. The monitor checks each running skill about once a second (`monitor.period_s`) and ends it, or wakes the planner, when something goes wrong.
- **System 1 — the learned policies.** Fast and reactive. Each running Skill drives its policy in a closed loop, from camera images and joint state to the next actions, at the policy's own rate (tens of Hz). The skill pushes those actions into a queue, and the controller merges what every running skill emits and forwards it to the robot every tick.

This split lets the robot be both *smart enough to plan* (slow, may fail, returns tool calls) and *fast enough to control* (closed-loop, reacting to every frame).
