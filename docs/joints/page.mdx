# Joints

Joints are the physical connections between two bodies in a robotic system. They 
define the relative positions and orientations of the two bodies and allow for 
relative motion between them.

A joint might have actuators and sensors attached to it, which are used to 
control the motion of the joint and measure its position and orientation.
