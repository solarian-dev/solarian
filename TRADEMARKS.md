# Solarian trademark policy

Solarian's code is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). The Solarian name and logo are not. This policy explains how you can use them.

The goal is simple: anyone should be able to tell what is made by the Solarian project and what is not. Most uses that are honest about that are allowed without asking.

## The marks

"Solarian"™ and the Solarian logo are trademarks of PrimaryKey Inc. This policy also covers the names of PrimaryKey's Solarian products: "Solarian Base", "Solarian Enterprise" and the Solarian model marketplace.

The Apache License does not grant any rights to these marks (section 6 of the license). This policy is the only permission to use them.

## Uses that need no permission

You may, without asking:

- **Refer to the project truthfully.** For example: "built with Solarian", "runs on Solarian", "a Solarian plugin", "integrates with Solarian".
- **Redistribute unmodified Solarian** under its own name, including inside a product.
- **Publish a plugin named `solarian-<name>`** (Python package `solarian_<name>`). This is the naming convention `solarian create-plugin` uses, and it is fine for independent plugins. Your plugin must not present itself as official or endorsed, and its README should say it is maintained independently of the Solarian project.
- **Write and teach about Solarian**: articles, tutorials, books, talks, courses, videos and research papers.
- **Run community groups and events**, such as meetups or online channels, if they are non-commercial and clearly unofficial.

## Uses that need written permission

Ask first before you:

- **Use a PrimaryKey product name.** Do not use "Solarian Base", "Solarian Enterprise" or the Solarian marketplace name, or a confusingly similar name, for your own product or service.
- **Claim official status.** Do not call anything "official", "certified" or "endorsed by Solarian". Only repositories under [github.com/solarian-dev](https://github.com/solarian-dev) and listings PrimaryKey designates as official may say this.
- **Name a modified version "Solarian".** A fork or modified distribution must use a different name. It may say it is "based on Solarian".
- **Use "Solarian" in a company, product, hosted service or domain name.** For example, a hosted service cannot be called "Solarian Cloud". "Acme Robotics Cloud, compatible with Solarian" is fine.
- **Sell merchandise** with the Solarian name or logo.

## Logo

You may use the unmodified Solarian logo to link to or refer to the project, for example on a page that lists supported frameworks. Do not alter the logo, combine it with your own marks, or use it as part of another product's branding without permission. Brand assets will be published here once available.

## Hardware

A robot, kit or component may say it "runs Solarian" or is "Solarian-compatible". It may not be branded as a Solarian robot, or as official or certified Solarian hardware, without permission.
